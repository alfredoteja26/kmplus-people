Product process the scorecard must fit

These rules were added so KMPlus People can run performance management without becoming Portaverse. The 2027 item list has to obey them. Deduced from CONTEXT.md and ADRs 0001–0004.

RuleWhat it means for the KPI list
KpiYear, not Cycle One annual container. Admin starts KpiPlanning, then KpiMonitoring, may open a tenant-wide KpiAdjustmentWindow (typical rhythm once a quarter, not automatic), then closes and scores the year. Only one year may be in planning or monitoring.
KpiPortfolio hangs off Assignment Five KpiItems live on the filled seat, not on the Person and not on a vacant Position. Dual-hat means two portfolios. Mid-year mutation leaves the old portfolio on the old Assignment and starts a new one.
Dual approval LineManager and Admin both approve the portfolio, in either order, before any KpiCheckIn. Any edit or Return clears both stamps. Check-Ins use the same dual stamp. Pending Check-Ins do not count at year close.
LineManager skips vacant seats Sales Associates today skip empty Sales Manager and report to Head of Sales (Puti). Consultants skip empty Project Manager and Head of Delivery and report to Operational Director (Rayhan). If no filled ancestor exists, Admin is the remaining approver (CEO’s own portfolio).
Admin is a User grant Not implied by HR. May stack with Employee or Manager so that Person still holds a KpiPortfolio. LineManager must be a different Person. Seed: Alfredo has the grant as Employee; Denny as HR does not.
Cascade is a parent pointer Every non-root KpiItem names one parent KpiItem. Direct is a raw sum and needs the same unit and CheckInFrequency (monthly or quarterly only). Indirect is alignment. Composite CEO workbook items must be split or marked Indirect.

What similar firms measure

KMPlus is a hybrid: human-capital software plus IT / consulting implementation. The closest published operating metrics come from professional-services research, not from BUMN kamus templates.

1. Balanced Scorecard is a strategy map, not a 108-row dictionary

Kaplan and Norton introduced the Balanced Scorecard so firms would measure more than last year’s finance numbers, then later added strategy maps: pick objectives first, metrics second. Cascading is supposed to localize measures, not copy the corporate list onto every role. Using the scorecard as a KPI warehouse is a named failure mode.

Kaplan, R. S. (2010). Local PDF. Original: Harvard Business School Working Paper 10-074. See also Balanced Scorecard Institute, cascading overview.

2. Professional-services firms run a short operating dashboard

Kantata’s professional-services scorecard (the PSA category KMPlus resembles on the delivery side) tracks forecast vs target, sold vs delivered margin, pipeline vs bookings, utilization, joiners/leavers, backlog, and book-to-bill. Those are company and practice measures. They are not ten personal KPIs for every consultant.

Kantata. Local PDF. Original: A Balanced Scorecard for Running a Professional Services Organization.

3. SPI Research: five KPIs that actually predict profit

The 2026 Professional Services Maturity Benchmark (509 firms, about 245,000 consultants) repeatedly names five predictors. 2025 industry averages vs Level-5 stretch:

Other SPI figures useful for the commercial pack: pipeline coverage about 175% of quarterly bookings in 2025 (high performers 224%); on-time delivery 73.4% in 2024 (so a 95% on-time target is ambitious); high-performing firms win 56.5% of bids vs 45.1% for the rest. The CEO’s 30% win-rate target is therefore conservative if the denominator is qualified proposals, aggressive if it includes every RFP.

Deltek summary of SPI 2026; 2026 PSO benchmarks; Local SPI 2025 PDF (268 pages); original Workday-hosted copy; SPI Research 2026 report page.

4. Software consultancies warn against utilization-as-success

Thoughtworks publishes the opposite of a utilization cult: govern by outcomes, assign one owner per objective, keep a handful of metrics, and treat DORA / velocity / utilization as diagnostics when outcomes lag. Google’s re:Work OKR guide: 3–5 objectives per level, about three key results each; OKRs are public; 60–70% attainment is the sweet spot; OKRs are not the performance evaluation.

Thoughtworks, North Star before the map; Five dysfunctions of an operating model; Google re:Work, Set goals with OKRs.

Implication for KMPlus. Use BSC perspectives to balance the company list. Take SPI’s five as the delivery-and-margin spine, then add Sales as two of the company five (pipeline and win rate / booked value) because KMPlus has a filled commercial practice. Use Thoughtworks / Google as the brake: five items, outcomes over activity. The CEO workbook’s 25 corporate rows stay Board context; they are not 25 employee KPIs.

Design options

Locked A. Position-first slim cascade

Five KpiItems per filled Assignment. Company five include Sales (pipeline and win rate / booked value) plus revenue, utilization, and delivery reliability. Parent pointer to one CEO or Director item. Indirect unless unit and CheckInFrequency match. SMART Values as one 10% manager-scored item. Vacant Positions have no KpiPortfolio.

Fits: KpiYear process, dual approval, LineManager skip-up, a 17-person firm, SPI plus a real Sales team.
Costs: the CEO dictionary is a source, not the uploaded list. Director/Head must be split into Operational vs Corporate Affairs packs.

2 B. Load the workbook as-is, then map families onto Positions

Preserve all 108 rows. Assign Sales pack to every sales seat, Consultant pack to consultants and BAs, generic Director pack to OD, CAD, and Head of Sales.

Fits: maximum fidelity to the CEO draft.
Costs: 9–10 items each, composite parents the app cannot Direct-sum, sprint cadences the app cannot store, scorecards for Product/Finance/HR/PM with no holder, CAD scored on delivery SLAs.

3 C. Company OKRs, personal KPIs stay tiny

Five to seven company OKRs for 2027 (revenue mix, pipeline, delivery reliability, product roadmap, capability). Individuals hold 3–4 committed KPIs used for the rating. Stretch work lives in OKRs and is not the bonus math.

Fits: Google re:Work; Thoughtworks outcome governance; product Phase 1.
Costs: two languages (OKR vs KPI) for a firm that asked for a PMS dictionary. Rating bands in the workbook assume KPI achievement, not 0.7 OKR grades.

Avoid D. Full Portaverse / BUMN clone

Three-level Impact/Output tree, calibration committee, 360, bonus curve, kamus-per-position IDs. The Pelindo skills already do this for a client. KMPlus People was specified to steal vocabulary, not that complexity.
