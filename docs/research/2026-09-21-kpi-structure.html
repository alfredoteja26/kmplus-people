What may be Direct

Direct is a raw sum. Child and parent must share unit and CheckInFrequency. That is why Sales booked IDR can roll up and win-rate percentages cannot.

May Direct-sumMust stay Indirect
Booked contract value in IDR, same monthly or quarterly window, children-only or own-plus-children on the parent Win rate, pipeline coverage (×), CSAT, SMART index, mixed composites such as CEO-04
Utilization % only if every child uses the same % definition and frequency On-time % mixed with defect counts; monthly child into quarterly parent
Default cascade mode is Indirect. Turn Direct on only after the units are identical. The 2027 first pass should mark Sales booked IDR as the only planned Direct chain.
