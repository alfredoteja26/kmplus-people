# KpiYear and KpiAdmin replace KpiCycle as the product

The product mixed planning and Check-Ins inside one user-facing Cycle. We replaced that feature with **KpiAdmin**: start **KpiPlanning**, start **KpiMonitoring**, open a tenant-wide **KpiAdjustmentWindow**, and close the year. **KpiYear** remains as the annual record so **KpiPortfolios** have a home; Cycle is not a product name.

We rejected renaming the screen only while keeping KpiCycle as the thing people operate, and rejected deleting the year container (a Portfolio still belongs to a year).
