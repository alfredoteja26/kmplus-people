# Home shows what needs you, by role

**Home** leads with a "Needs you" strip built from the signed-in User's roles and grants, then shows that Person's own week. An Employee sees their KpiPortfolio waiting for approval or a KpiCheckIn due. A LineManager sees KpiPortfolios and KpiCheckIns waiting for their approval. An Admin sees late KpiCheckIns and current Assignments without a KpiPortfolio. HR sees Curricula Vitae waiting for review. Each item is a count and one link to the page that clears it; an empty strip collapses to one line.

We rejected keeping Home strictly personal. On the seeded Tenant, two of the three working roles landed on an empty page while their real work waited elsewhere. We rejected a company dashboard on Home: the strip only counts work the viewer can act on, and Admin's company reports stay on Project Admin.
