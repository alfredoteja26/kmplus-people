# Load may show overload

**Load** on one **Staffing** row is a whole percent from 1 to 100. The sum of a Person’s Loads may exceed 100. That excess is overload, and the product shows it.

We rejected a rule that blocks a sum above 100. The overload is the fact the screen has to show. We rejected keeping each old percent on its own row. Admin edits the number in place.
